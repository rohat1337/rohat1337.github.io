How are the units counted?

Everything is counted from today up to the start of the day you picked. Sleeps are the nights in between. Mondays and weekends are the Mondays and Saturdays still to come. Paydays are the 25ths of the month. Full moons are calculated from the Moon’s average cycle of 29.53 days. Coffees, haircuts and heartbeats are rough estimates, marked with ≈.

Can I change or delete a countdown?

Open it and tap the ••• button in the top right corner.

What happens when the day arrives?

It says “It’s today”. After that it moves to “Already happened” and counts the days since.
