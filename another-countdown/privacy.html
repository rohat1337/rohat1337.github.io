Another Countdown

Privacy Policy · Effective October 9, 2026

Another Countdown is a countdown app. It does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Your countdowns stay on your iPhone

The names and dates you add are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you tap the share button, the app makes an image of the countdown and passes it to the app you choose. What happens after that is up to you and that app.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
