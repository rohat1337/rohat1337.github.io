Questions

Does Albumhouse come with music?
No. It plays music from a server you run or are allowed to use. It doesn't sell, stream, or find music for you.
What do I need?
An iPhone and a Jellyfin, Navidrome, or other OpenSubsonic server with your music on it, reachable from your phone.
What's the server address?
The address you open your server with in a browser, for example http://192.168.1.20:8096 for Jellyfin or http://192.168.1.20:4533 for Navidrome. Use HTTPS if your server has it.
Can I use it away from home?
Yes, if your server is reachable from outside your network, for example through a VPN or a domain with HTTPS.
Does it download music for offline listening?
Not yet. Albumhouse streams from your server.
Is it free?
Yes. Browsing, search, playback, the queue, favorites, and playlists are free.
