Tidy

Privacy Policy · Effective October 10, 2026

Tidy is a photo cleanup app. It does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

Your photos

With your permission, Tidy reads your photo library on your iPhone to find duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, large videos and blurry photos. Your photos are analyzed on the device and never leave it. You can give Tidy access to all photos or only the ones you choose, and change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

To make later scans faster, Tidy stores small analysis results (such as image fingerprints and file sizes) inside the app on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Deleting

Tidy only deletes photos you select and confirm in the iPhone’s own dialog. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos and can be recovered for 30 days.

Purchases

The optional Tidy Pro purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Tidy receives only confirmation that the purchase is valid; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Tidy does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
