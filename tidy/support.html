I deleted something by mistake

Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, select it and tap Recover. Deleted items stay there for 30 days.

How does Tidy decide what to keep?

In each group it suggests a favorite first, then an edited photo, then the highest resolution and sharpest. You can pick another one before deleting. Favorites are never selected for deletion automatically, and hidden photos are never scanned.

Why weren’t some photos grouped as similar?

Tidy is strict by default: it only groups near-identical photos taken within a minute of each other. In Pro you can choose Balanced or Loose to group more.

What does Pro include, and is it a subscription?

Pro is a one-time purchase. It removes the 100-a-day deletion limit and unlocks similar-photo sensitivity, blurry photo cleanup and large-video cleanup. There is no subscription. If you reinstall or get a new iPhone, use Restore Purchases in Settings.

Some photos were skipped

Photos stored only in iCloud need to download first. If you were offline, Tidy skips them and checks them on the next scan.
