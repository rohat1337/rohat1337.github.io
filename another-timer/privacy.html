Another Timer

Privacy Policy · Effective October 8, 2026

Another Timer is a focus timer. It does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Your sessions stay on your iPhone

What you plan, what you actually did, and your session history are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone for when a session ends. Nothing is sent through a server.

Standup

If you tap “Write my standup”, the app uses Apple Intelligence to write a short summary of your sessions. This runs on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device model. Your sessions are not sent to us. Apple’s handling of Apple Intelligence is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If you use a share button, the text goes wherever you choose to send it.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
