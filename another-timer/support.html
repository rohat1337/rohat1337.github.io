Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Email rohat.upl@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How does it work?

Write what you’re going to do, pick 15, 25 or 50 minutes and tap Start. When the time is up, or you tap Stop, the app asks what you actually did: exactly that, something else, or honestly, scrolled.

Does stopping early count?

Yes, as long as at least one minute went by. It’s marked as stopped early.

I don’t get a notification when time is up

Check Settings → Notifications → Another Timer and make sure notifications are allowed.

The standup button is missing

It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
