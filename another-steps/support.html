It says 0 steps

The app reads your steps from Apple Health. Check that access is on: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Another Steps, and turn on Steps and Walking + Running Distance. Your iPhone counts steps automatically when it’s with you.

Where do the comparisons come from?

Each place has a real length or height: the Golden Gate Bridge is 2,737 m, the Eiffel Tower 330 m, the Titanic 269 m, Mount Everest 8,849 m. The app picks the ones where your distance lands closest to a few times the length, so the numbers feel concrete. Tap the sign to see others.

What does “all time” mean?

Everything Apple Health has recorded for you, measured against a trip around the Earth (40,075 km), and after that, the Moon.

Is this a medical app?

No. It’s for fun and motivation. For health advice, talk to a professional.

Where is my data stored?

It stays in Apple Health on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
