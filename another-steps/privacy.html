Another Steps

Privacy Policy · Effective October 9, 2026

Another Steps is a step counter. It does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads two things from Apple Health: your step count and your walking and running distance. It only reads; it never writes to Health. The data is used on your iPhone to show your steps and turn them into comparisons. It is not stored by the app, not sent to us or anyone else, and never used for advertising or sold.

You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Another Steps.

Sharing

If you tap the share button, the app makes an image of today’s steps and passes it to the app you choose. What happens after that is up to you and that app.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
