Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Albumhouse is an iPhone app that plays music from a server you run or are allowed to use, such as Jellyfin or Navidrome. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: we don't collect your listening history, your music library, or any personal data. The app talks to your music server, and to Apple only for what Apple provides to every app.

Who we are

Albumhouse is published by Rohat Bedikanli. Contact: rohat.upl@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Albumhouse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own that your data passes through.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your sign-in: the server address, your user name on that server, and a sign-in token, stored in your iPhone's Keychain on this device only. Your password is not saved. For Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers, the token is derived from your password, as that protocol requires.
  • A saved copy of your library: album covers and library pages, so the app opens quickly and works during short outages. You can clear them in Settings → Storage. iOS may also remove them when space is low.
  • Your queue and display preferences: the current queue and position, shuffle and repeat, and how albums are sorted.

Signing out or switching sources deletes the sign-in, saved library, covers, and queue for that server. Deleting the app removes everything.

What goes to your music server

To work, the app sends your server the requests any music client does: signing in, listing albums, searching, streaming audio, reading and changing favorites and playlists, and reporting what you played so your server's "recently played" stays accurate. That information is handled by your server under its own settings and, if someone else runs it, their policies. Music streams directly from your server to your iPhone.

What goes to Apple

  • Crash reports and usage statistics: only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings. Apple then gives us crash reports and aggregated, anonymous statistics such as installs and sessions. They don't contain your server, account, or music.
  • Purchases: if Albumhouse offers in-app purchases, Apple processes them. We receive no payment details, only whether a purchase is active.

When you contact us

If you send feedback from Settings → Send Feedback or email us, we receive your email address and what you write. The app adds only its version, your iOS version, and the kind of server (Jellyfin or OpenSubsonic), never your server address, user name, or library. We use your message only to answer and improve the app, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Tracking, sharing, and selling

Albumhouse does not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data. There are no third-party SDKs in the app.

Children

Albumhouse is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you apart from emails you send us, there is nothing else to access or delete. Ask us to delete your emails at any time. Data on your iPhone is under your control: sign out or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so first, and the App Store privacy details will be updated.
