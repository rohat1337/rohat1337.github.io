Another One

Privacy Policy · Effective October 8, 2026

Another One is a to-do app. It does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Your tasks stay on your iPhone

Your tasks, notes, grades and past days are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Performance review

If you tap “Review my day”, the app uses Apple Intelligence to write a short review of your day. This runs on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device model. Your tasks are not sent to us. Apple’s handling of Apple Intelligence is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If you use the share button on the report card, the text goes wherever you choose to send it. That is up to you and the app you share to.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
