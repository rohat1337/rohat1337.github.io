How do I mark something as stuck or dropped?

Swipe left on the task. You can also press and hold it for a menu.

How is the grade calculated?

Done tasks count fully. Dropped tasks with a reason count half. Stuck tasks with a note count a little. Everything is divided by the number of tasks on today’s list.

What does “End the day” do?

It saves today’s grade, clears finished and dropped tasks, and keeps stuck and untouched tasks for tomorrow.

The performance review button is missing

It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

Where are my tasks stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
